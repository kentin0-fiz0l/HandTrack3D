## Tips for Success

### Finding Good Testers

**Good testers**:
✅ Have webcams
✅ Interested in web tech
✅ Give honest feedback
✅ Actually follow through

**Avoid**:
❌ People who always say "yes" but don't do it
❌ Those who give only positive feedback
❌ Non-technical users (for this beta)

### Response Rates

**Expected**:
- Personal contacts: 60-80% acceptance
- Community posts: 5-10% engagement
- Total responses needed: 15-20 invitations for 10 testers

### Timing

**Best times to post**:
- Twitter/X: 9-11 AM or 2-4 PM (your timezone)
- Reddit: 2-4 PM EST weekdays
- Discord: Evening (7-9 PM)
- Email: Morning (8-10 AM)
